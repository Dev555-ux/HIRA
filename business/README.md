# business/

**Purpose**: Non-technical startup material — market research, pricing, customer validation findings, and go-to-market thinking.

**Will eventually contain**:
- Customer segment research (the six verticals under evaluation, and findings per vertical)
- Pricing model notes and experiments
- Sales/outreach scripts and learnings
- Competitive landscape notes
- Unit economics (CAC, retention assumptions, etc.) once there's real data

**Why it exists**: HIRA is a business, not just a codebase. Technical decisions (e.g., which vertical to optimize prompts for) should be informed by what's in this folder, and vice versa — this is where market reality lives.
