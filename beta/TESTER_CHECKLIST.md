# HIRA Beta Tester Checklist

Thanks for trying HIRA. This should take about 15–20 minutes. Go through it in order — don't skip around. There's no wrong way to break something; if it's confusing or broken, that's exactly what we need to hear about (see the feedback form at the end).

## 1. Authentication

- [ ] Create your account
- [ ] Log out
- [ ] Log back in
- [ ] Note: did staying logged in / logging back in feel normal, or did anything surprise you?

## 2. Onboarding

- [ ] Complete the onboarding steps (business name, industry, etc.)
- [ ] Once you land on the dashboard, refresh the page — confirm you're still there and nothing reset
- [ ] Note: was anything asked that you didn't understand, or wished it explained more?

## 3. Demo Chat

Open Demo Chat and ask it, one at a time:

- [ ] "What services do you offer?"
- [ ] "What are your business hours?"
- [ ] "Can I book an appointment?"
- [ ] "What is your pricing?"
- [ ] "Tell me about my uploaded documents." (do this one *after* Part 4 below)
- [ ] Then ask it something **real** — an actual question one of your customers has asked you before. This is the most important message you'll send all session.

## 4. Business Knowledge

- [ ] Upload at least one real document or fact about your business (a policy, your hours, pricing, an FAQ — whatever you actually have)
- [ ] Go back to Demo Chat and ask a question that only that document could answer
- [ ] Check: did it answer correctly, using your actual information — not a generic guess?

## 5. CRM

- [ ] Create a lead (a fake or real prospective customer)
- [ ] Update that lead (change its stage, add a note)
- [ ] Search for it by name
- [ ] Delete it

## 6. General

- [ ] Look around the rest of the dashboard (Analytics, Settings, Calendar) for a couple of minutes
- [ ] Note anything that felt **confusing**
- [ ] Note anything that felt **broken**
- [ ] Note anything that felt **unexpectedly good** — this matters as much as the problems

## When you're done

Fill out the feedback form (you'll get a link separately) while it's fresh — same day if possible. If anything broke outright, use the bug report format instead of (or in addition to) the feedback form.
