# beta/

**Purpose**: Everything needed to run HIRA's first external beta (5–10 users) and turn their usage into evidence-based product decisions, not anecdotes.

**Contains**:
- [`FOUNDER_BETA_TRACKER.md`](FOUNDER_BETA_TRACKER.md) — the master dashboard; everything else in this folder rolls up into it
- [`SUCCESS_METRICS.md`](SUCCESS_METRICS.md) — what "the beta worked" actually means, in numbers
- [`TESTER_CHECKLIST.md`](TESTER_CHECKLIST.md) — the 15–20 minute first-run walkthrough every beta tester should complete
- [`BETA_TEST_SCRIPT.md`](BETA_TEST_SCRIPT.md) — a deeper, 30–45 minute session script that specifically exercises Business Knowledge grounding and how HIRA handles actions, incomplete requests, and confirmation
- [`FEEDBACK_FORM.md`](FEEDBACK_FORM.md) — the questions to ask after they've used it
- [`BUG_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`](BUG_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md) — reusable bug template
- [`FEATURE_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md`](FEATURE_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md) — reusable feature-request template
- [`AI_FAILURE_LOG.md`](AI_FAILURE_LOG.md) — the most important document here: where HIRA's *answers*, not the product, were wrong
- [`TRACKING_BOARD.md`](TRACKING_BOARD.md) — the spreadsheet/board schema, plus a ready-to-import `tracking_board.csv`
- [`PRIORITIZATION_FRAMEWORK.md`](PRIORITIZATION_FRAMEWORK.md) — how every piece of feedback gets triaged
- [`FOUNDER_PLAYBOOK.md`](FOUNDER_PLAYBOOK.md) — the operating rhythm for running the beta day to day
- [`POST_BETA_SUMMARY.md`](POST_BETA_SUMMARY.md) — the end-of-beta report template, filled in from evidence in the files above
- [`DEMO_BUSINESS_KNOWLEDGE_VELOCITY_LUXE_MOTORS.md`](DEMO_BUSINESS_KNOWLEDGE_VELOCITY_LUXE_MOTORS.md) — a fictional, fully cross-checked Business Knowledge document (luxury car dealership) for testers without their own content ready yet

**Why it exists**: A 5–10 person beta produces a small amount of high-value signal and a large amount of noise (one person's pet peeve, one person's unrepresentative workflow). Without a structure decided in advance, it's easy to either overreact to the loudest tester or under-react to a real pattern buried across three quiet ones. This folder is that structure, decided *before* the first tester logs in — not improvised while reading feedback.
