## 1. Company Overview

Velocity Luxe Motors is a multi-brand luxury and performance automobile house headquartered in DLF Cyber City, Gurugram, Haryana. Founded in 2012 by a small group of automotive enthusiasts and former manufacturer-side executives, the dealership began as a single-brand Porsche and Audi retail partner before growing, over the following decade, into one of North India's most recognised destinations for ultra-luxury and performance vehicles.

**Founding story.** The company was started by Arjun Malhotra, a former regional operations lead at a European luxury importer, together with two co-founders who had backgrounds in private banking and precision engineering. The trio identified a gap in the Delhi NCR market: buyers of six- and seven-figure automobiles were being served with the same transactional approach used for mass-market vehicles. Velocity Luxe Motors was built around a different premise — that buying a car of this calibre should feel closer to a private client relationship than a retail transaction. The first showroom opened on a modest 8,000 square foot plot in 2012 with a single Porsche and Audi franchise. By 2016, the dealership had added Mercedes-Benz and BMW, and by 2019 had been awarded authorised dealership status for Bentley and Aston Martin. Rolls-Royce, Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, and Land Rover followed between 2020 and 2023, culminating in the current flagship campus.

**Vision.** To be the most trusted name in luxury and performance motoring across North India, recognised as much for the ownership experience that follows a sale as for the sale itself.

**Mission.** To give every client — whether purchasing their first premium sedan or their fifth hypercar — a buying journey that is transparent, unhurried, and genuinely informed, backed by service infrastructure that protects the value and enjoyment of their vehicle for as long as they own it.

**Core values.** Velocity Luxe Motors operates on four stated principles that guide staff training and client interactions: *Transparency* (no hidden costs, clear timelines, honest answers about waitlists and allocation), *Craftsmanship* (respect for the engineering and heritage behind every marque represented), *Discretion* (client privacy is treated as a professional obligation, not a courtesy), and *Longevity* (the relationship is expected to outlast the first sale, spanning service, upgrades, and eventually trade-in or resale).

**Customer-first philosophy.** Sales consultants at Velocity Luxe Motors are trained not to lead with pricing or promotions but with a genuine needs assessment — usage patterns, family requirements, existing vehicle collection, and long-term ownership goals — before recommending a specific model or brand. Consultants are salaried with a performance component tied to client satisfaction scores as well as sales volume, a structure the founders adopted deliberately to reduce pressure-selling.

**Showroom experience.** The flagship campus spans four connected pavilions across roughly 45,000 square feet, each pavilion styled to reflect the character of the brands it houses (a British heritage pavilion for Rolls-Royce, Bentley, and Aston Martin; an Italian performance pavilion for Ferrari, Lamborghini, and McLaren; a German engineering pavilion for Porsche, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, and Audi; and a dedicated Land Rover off-road experience zone with a small indoor test incline). A private client lounge on the upper floor offers consultation rooms, a coffee and refreshment bar, and a dedicated area for financing and documentation discussions away from the showroom floor.

**Luxury buying experience.** Clients purchasing flagship models (Rolls-Royce, Bentley Flying Spur, Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, and factory-bespoke orders) are assigned a dedicated Relationship Manager who remains their single point of contact from first enquiry through delivery and into the ownership period, coordinating financing, insurance, registration, and customisation on the client's behalf so they are not required to manage each vendor separately.

**Relationship with manufacturers.** Velocity Luxe Motors holds direct authorised dealership agreements with all eleven brands it represents, meaning vehicles are sourced through official manufacturer allocation channels rather than grey-market import, and all warranty and service work is manufacturer-backed. Several senior staff attend annual manufacturer training programmes in Europe and the United Kingdom to maintain brand certification.

**Sustainability initiatives.** The showroom campus runs on a hybrid solar-grid power system installed in 2021, covering roughly 40% of daytime energy use. The service centre uses a closed-loop water recycling system for the vehicle wash bays, and all authorised battery replacements (for hybrid and EV models such as the Porsche Taycan and Mercedes-Benz EQS) are routed through certified recyclers rather than general waste.

**Community involvement.** Velocity Luxe Motors sponsors an annual concours-style heritage drive in partnership with a Gurugram-based classic car club, and contributes vehicles and drivers to two road-safety awareness drives conducted with local traffic police each year. The dealership also runs a small scholarship programme for two apprentice technicians per year, sponsoring their manufacturer-certified training.

**Awards and recognitions.** Velocity Luxe Motors was recognised as "Luxury Dealership of the Year — North India" by the Automotive Retail Association of India in 2021 and 2023, and received a manufacturer-level "Circle of Excellence" service award from one of its European partner brands in 2022 for customer satisfaction scores in the top decile nationally.

**Why customers choose Velocity Luxe Motors.** Clients consistently cite three reasons in feedback surveys: the breadth of brands available under one roof (reducing the need to visit separate single-brand dealerships across the city), the continuity of a single Relationship Manager across the ownership lifecycle, and the depth of the in-house service and customisation infrastructure, which means most post-sale needs — from ceramic coating to warranty claims — are handled on the same campus where the vehicle was purchased.

## 2. Brands

### Rolls-Royce

Velocity Luxe Motors has held authorised Rolls-Royce dealership status since 2020, operating from a dedicated pavilion finished in the marque's traditional presentation style. Rolls-Royce represents the pinnacle of the dealership's offering — vehicles built substantially to individual commission rather than sold from standing stock. History: Rolls-Royce has been synonymous with hand-built luxury since 1904, and the modern range (Ghost, Cullinan, Phantom) continues that tradition under BMW Group ownership since 1998, with final assembly at Goodwood, England. Brand positioning: the most exclusive marque represented, positioned above even Bentley in price and bespoke depth. Customer profile: business owners, senior promoters of listed companies, and collectors, typically already owning two or more luxury vehicles. Popular models: Ghost (the marque's most accessible model), Cullinan (SUV, strong demand among younger promoters), and Phantom (flagship saloon, frequently chauffeur-specified). Typical price range: ₹8.5 crore to ₹13 crore ex-showroom depending on bespoke content. Ownership experience: every Rolls-Royce client is assigned a dedicated "Bespoke Collective" specialist who manages the personal commissioning process, which can include custom paint mixing, embroidered headrests, and starlight headliner configuration. Key differentiators: the "Magic Carpet Ride" air suspension system, unmatched interior hand-finishing, and near-silent cabin engineering. Frequently asked buying questions include waitlist timing (bespoke orders typically run 8–12 months from confirmation to delivery), whether test drives are available (yes, by private appointment only), and whether registration-ready stock exists (a small stock allocation of Ghost and Cullinan is maintained for clients who do not wish to wait for a bespoke build).

### Bentley

An authorised Bentley partner since 2019, Velocity Luxe Motors represents the full current range: Continental GT, Bentayga, and Flying Spur. History: Bentley was founded in 1919 and has combined motorsport pedigree (five Le Mans wins) with handcrafted luxury since. Brand positioning: sits just below Rolls-Royce in exclusivity but with a noticeably sportier, more driver-focused character. Customer profile: executives and entrepreneurs who want a car they can drive themselves at speed as well as be chauffeured in. Popular models: Continental GT (grand tourer, the brand's design and sales flagship), Bentayga (SUV, strongest seller in India), Flying Spur (four-door saloon, popular for chauffeur use). Typical price range: ₹3.8 crore to ₹4.5 crore ex-showroom. Ownership experience: Bentley's "Mulliner" personalisation programme is available through the dealership for clients wanting beyond-catalogue paint, leather, and trim combinations. Key differentiators: the powerful twin-turbo V8 shared across the current range and the "Naim for Bentley" audio system, along with the marque's signature diamond-quilted leather work. Frequently asked buying questions include the difference between Bentayga trims (S versus Azure versus EWB long-wheelbase), whether the plug-in hybrid Bentayga variant is available in India (yes, on indent order), and typical delivery timelines for factory-order specification (4–6 months).

### Ferrari

Velocity Luxe Motors became an authorised Ferrari dealership in 2022, representing Roma, 296 GTB, and Portofino. History: founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1947, the marque remains the most storied name in motorsport-derived road cars, with a Formula One programme running continuously since 1950. Brand positioning: the definitive Italian supercar brand, positioned on pure performance and racing pedigree rather than chauffeur-oriented luxury. Customer profile: passionate drivers, often existing performance-car owners, ranging from first-time supercar buyers to established collectors. Popular models: Roma (front-engine grand tourer, the most accessible current model), 296 GTB (mid-engine plug-in hybrid V6, the brand's technological showcase), Portofino (convertible grand tourer, frequently available Certified Pre-Owned). Typical price range: ₹4.8 crore (Certified Pre-Owned Portofino) to ₹6.2 crore (new 296 GTB) ex-showroom. Ownership experience: Ferrari ownership includes complimentary scheduled maintenance for the first three years and invitation to the marque's owner-only track day programme, coordinated through the dealership twice yearly at the Buddh International Circuit. Key differentiators: naturally aspirated and hybrid V6/V8 engineering pedigree, Ferrari's Formula One-derived steering wheel controls, and the "Tailor Made" personalisation atelier. Frequently asked buying questions include allocation timing for new models (typically 6–10 months from order confirmation), whether left-hand-drive models can be imported (no — all Velocity Luxe Motors Ferraris are right-hand-drive, India-market specification), and Certified Pre-Owned inspection standards (190-point manufacturer-certified inspection).

### Lamborghini

An authorised Lamborghini partner since 2022, the dealership represents Huracán EVO and Urus. History: founded in 1963 by Ferruccio Lamborghini, the brand built its identity on naturally aspirated V10/V12 engineering and dramatic design language, and has been part of the Volkswagen Group (via Audi) since 1998. Brand positioning: the boldest, most visually uncompromising marque in the portfolio, positioned for buyers who want maximum presence. Customer profile: younger promoters and entrepreneurs, frequently first-generation wealth, often purchasing their first true supercar. Popular models: Urus (Super SUV, the brand's highest-volume model in India by a wide margin), Huracán EVO (V10 supercar, the emotional flagship of the range in showroom). Typical price range: ₹4.1 crore (Huracán EVO) to ₹4.4 crore (Urus) ex-showroom. Ownership experience: Lamborghini's "Ad Personam" programme allows near-unlimited exterior colour and interior trim personalisation, coordinated through the dealership's design consultants. Key differentiators: naturally aspirated V10 engine note (increasingly rare in the segment), all-wheel-drive standard across the range, and the most aggressive design language among represented brands. Frequently asked buying questions include Urus versus Bentayga/Cayenne comparisons (a very common cross-shop question), whether a manual gearbox is available (no — the current range is exclusively automatic/dual-clutch), and typical waitlist for Urus (3–5 months for standard specification, longer for bespoke colour).

### Porsche

Velocity Luxe Motors' founding franchise, representing Porsche since 2012. Current range on display: 911 Carrera, Cayenne, Panamera, and Taycan. History: founded in 1931 by Ferdinand Porsche, with the 911 in continuous production since 1963, making it the longest continuously produced sports car in the world. Brand positioning: the "everyday supercar" — genuine performance credentials with daily usability, sitting a step below the true exotic marques on price. Customer profile: the widest customer base of any brand represented, from first-time luxury buyers upgrading from premium German sedans to serious collectors adding a 911 to an existing garage. Popular models: Cayenne (SUV, consistently the dealership's highest-volume model across all brands), 911 Carrera (icon status, strongest brand loyalty of any model on the floor), Taycan (the dealership's first mainstream EV offering). Typical price range: ₹1.4 crore (Cayenne) to ₹1.9 crore (911 Carrera). Ownership experience: Porsche's "Tequipment" and "Exclusive Manufaktur" programmes allow factory-level customisation; the dealership also runs a quarterly Porsche driving experience day. Key differentiators: rear-engine 911 layout, motorsport-derived PDK dual-clutch transmission, and the broadest model range (sedan, SUV, sports car, EV) under one badge. Frequently asked buying questions include 911 Carrera versus Carrera S trim differences, Taycan real-world range in Delhi NCR conditions (approximately 400–420 km on a full charge for the standard range variant), and Cayenne hybrid availability (yes, on indent).

### Aston Martin

Authorised Aston Martin dealer since 2019, representing DB12 and Vantage. History: founded in 1913, with a design philosophy built around hand-finished British grand touring and a long, well-known association with the James Bond film franchise. Brand positioning: British alternative to Ferrari — similarly performance-focused but with a more restrained, tailored design language. Customer profile: design-conscious buyers who want exclusivity (Aston Martin volumes are deliberately low) and are often already familiar with the brand through film and motorsport culture. Popular models: DB12 (grand tourer, positioned as the brand's most complete current model), Vantage (sports car, the more overtly aggressive of the two). Typical price range: ₹3.5 crore (Vantage) to ₹4.5 crore (DB12). Ownership experience: Aston Martin's "Q by Aston Martin" bespoke programme is available for paint, leather, and trim personalisation beyond the standard configurator. Key differentiators: hand-stitched interiors, a twin-turbocharged V8 sourced from Mercedes-AMG under a long-standing technical partnership, and design continuity with the brand's film heritage. Frequently asked buying questions include DB12 versus Bentley Continental GT comparisons, expected annual volumes in India (low — typically single digits per year through this dealership, reinforcing exclusivity), and whether manual servicing intervals differ from other brands (broadly similar 12-month/10,000 km intervals).

### McLaren

Authorised McLaren dealer since 2023, the newest brand addition, representing GT and Artura. History: McLaren Automotive was established in 2010 as the road-car division of the McLaren Formula One racing team, giving every model direct motorsport engineering lineage. Brand positioning: the most track-focused, engineering-led marque represented, appealing to buyers who prioritise driving dynamics above all else. Customer profile: experienced enthusiast drivers, frequently with existing track-day experience, who view the car as much as a driving instrument as a luxury statement. Popular models: Artura (plug-in hybrid V6, the brand's current technological flagship), GT (the more comfort-oriented grand tourer variant). Typical price range: ₹3.9 crore (GT) to ₹4.3 crore (Artura). Ownership experience: McLaren buyers are offered a factory-style carbon-fibre monocoque construction briefing at delivery, explaining the engineering rationale behind the car's construction, and access to McLaren's track-day partner programme at the Buddh International Circuit. Key differentiators: full carbon-fibre monocoque chassis (unique among represented brands at this price point), lightweight-first engineering philosophy, and direct Formula One technology transfer. Frequently asked buying questions include Artura versus Huracán EVO comparisons, real-world usability for daily driving (McLarens have historically had firmer low-speed ride quality, though the Artura is noted internally as more compliant than prior generations), and after-sales infrastructure maturity (the newest brand on the floor, so the dealership is transparent that some specialist parts carry slightly longer lead times than more established brands).

### Mercedes-Benz

An authorised Mercedes-Benz dealer since 2016, representing S-Class, G-Class, AMG GT, and EQS. History: Mercedes-Benz traces its lineage to Karl Benz's 1886 patent for the first practical automobile, making it the oldest automotive brand in continuous operation globally. Brand positioning: the benchmark for mainstream luxury, sitting a tier below the exotic marques but offering the deepest engineering and technology heritage. Customer profile: the broadest customer base after Porsche — corporate buyers, senior executives, and families seeking flagship luxury with strong resale value. Popular models: S-Class (long regarded as the technological flagship of the luxury sedan segment), G-Class/G63 AMG (SUV, exceptionally strong demand and typically the dealership's longest waitlist), AMG GT (performance coupe). Typical price range: ₹1.7 crore (EQS) to ₹3.3 crore (G63 AMG). Ownership experience: Mercedes-Benz clients have access to the dealership's "Mercedes me" connected services onboarding and a complimentary first-service pickup and drop. Key differentiators: the S-Class's benchmark ride comfort and technology suite, the G-Class's boxy design continuity spanning over four decades largely unchanged in silhouette, and AMG's in-house performance engineering. Frequently asked buying questions include G63 AMG waitlist timing (typically the longest in the dealership's entire inventory — 10–14 months is common), EQS real-world range (approximately 580–600 km WLTP-rated, with Delhi NCR real-world figures closer to 480–520 km depending on climate control use), and S-Class long-wheelbase versus standard-wheelbase availability (long-wheelbase is the dominant India-market specification and the only variant typically stocked).

### BMW

An authorised BMW dealer since 2016, representing 7 Series, X7, and M5. History: founded in 1916, originally as an aircraft engine manufacturer before transitioning to motorcycles and then automobiles, with the "M" performance division established in 1972. Brand positioning: positioned as the driver-focused alternative to Mercedes-Benz within the mainstream luxury tier. Customer profile: executives and business owners who prioritise driving engagement alongside luxury, along with strong demand from buyers upgrading from the brand's mid-range models. Popular models: X7 (flagship SUV, strong family-buyer demand), 7 Series (flagship sedan, increasingly ordered with the plug-in hybrid variant), M5 (performance sedan, a smaller but dedicated enthusiast following). Typical price range: ₹1.3 crore (X7) to ₹1.9 crore (7 Series/M5). Ownership experience: BMW's "Individual" programme allows expanded paint and interior personalisation beyond standard configurator options. Key differentiators: the M5's twin-turbo V8 and all-wheel-drive performance credentials, the 7 Series's rear-seat entertainment and comfort package (a significant differentiator for chauffeur-driven buyers), and BMW's driver-assistance technology suite. Frequently asked buying questions include 7 Series petrol versus plug-in hybrid variant differences, X7 six-seat versus seven-seat configuration availability, and M5 track-use suitability (track-capable but the dealership recommends the Porsche 911 or a McLaren for buyers whose primary use case is circuit driving).

### Audi

An authorised Audi dealer since 2012 (the dealership's other founding franchise alongside Porsche), representing A8 L, Q8, and RS7. History: Audi's four-ring badge represents the 1932 merger of four German manufacturers, and the brand has been part of the Volkswagen Group since 1966, known for pioneering quattro all-wheel-drive technology. Brand positioning: the more understated, technology-led alternative within the mainstream luxury tier, popular with buyers who prefer subtlety over overt brand signalling. Customer profile: professionals and executives, notably strong among buyers in technology, consulting, and finance sectors. Popular models: Q8 (flagship SUV coupe, the dealership's best-selling Audi model), A8 L (long-wheelbase flagship sedan, popular for chauffeur use), RS7 (performance estate-coupe, a niche but loyal following). Typical price range: ₹1.05 crore (Q8) to ₹1.95 crore (RS7). Ownership experience: Audi's "Audi Exclusive" programme offers extended personalisation, and the dealership provides a dedicated Audi Virtual Cockpit and MMI technology walkthrough at delivery given the brand's technology-forward interior systems. Key differentiators: quattro all-wheel-drive standard across the represented range, understated exterior design relative to competitors, and class-leading cabin technology integration. Frequently asked buying questions include Q8 versus X7/GLE comparisons, quattro system explanation for first-time all-wheel-drive buyers, and RS7 real-world running costs relative to the standard A7 range (meaningfully higher due to performance tyre and brake wear).

### Land Rover

An authorised Land Rover dealer since 2023, representing Range Rover, Range Rover Sport, and Defender 110. History: Land Rover was founded in 1948 and has built its identity on genuine off-road engineering credibility combined, in the modern Range Rover line, with flagship luxury positioning. Brand positioning: the only true luxury off-road specialist represented, appealing to buyers who want genuine all-terrain capability alongside flagship comfort. Customer profile: buyers with second homes or frequent travel to hill or rural destinations, along with urban buyers who value the commanding driving position and road presence. Popular models: Range Rover (flagship, the brand's strongest seller by value), Range Rover Sport (more driver-focused variant), Defender 110 (genuine off-road specialist, popular with a younger, adventure-oriented buyer segment). Typical price range: ₹1.2 crore (Defender 110) to ₹2.9 crore (Range Rover). Ownership experience: the dealership's indoor test incline and off-road demonstration zone is unique to Land Rover among the represented brands, allowing genuine capability demonstration on-site rather than requiring an off-site venue. Key differentiators: Terrain Response adaptive off-road systems, genuine wading depth and articulation capability validated on-site, and the Range Rover's continued flagship positioning within the luxury SUV segment. Frequently asked buying questions include Range Rover versus Range Rover Sport differences, Defender 110 versus Defender 90 (only the 110 five-door is currently stocked; the 90 three-door is available on indent order), and real-world reliability perception (the dealership addresses this proactively, noting that its extended warranty and service contract options are specifically popular among Land Rover buyers for this reason).

## 4. Buying Journey

Velocity Luxe Motors follows a structured but unhurried buying process, typically spanning between two weeks (for in-stock vehicles) and twelve months (for bespoke Rolls-Royce or waitlisted models).

**Step 1 — Initial consultation (Day 1).** A prospective buyer meets with a sales consultant or, for flagship brands, a dedicated Relationship Manager, either in the showroom or via a scheduled video call. This session focuses on understanding usage patterns, existing vehicle ownership, budget range, and timeline rather than pushing a specific model.

**Step 2 — Vehicle recommendation (Day 1–3).** Based on the consultation, the consultant proposes one to three suitable models, drawing from in-stock inventory or discussing factory-order options where the client's preferences fall outside current stock.

**Step 3 — Test drive (Day 3–7).** A test drive is scheduled — see Section 8 for full test drive policy. Most clients test drive one to two vehicles before narrowing their decision.

**Step 4 — Reservation (Day 7–10).** Once a vehicle is selected, a reservation is placed with a booking amount (see Section 14 for booking amount policy) that secures either an in-stock vehicle or a production slot for a factory order.

**Step 5 — Financing (Day 10–20).** For clients requiring financing, the finance team begins loan processing in parallel with other steps (see Section 5). Cash purchases skip this step.

**Step 6 — Insurance (Day 15–25).** The insurance team prepares policy options, typically finalised alongside financing so the insurance policy is ready at delivery (see Section 6).

**Step 7 — Documentation (Day 15–30).** KYC documentation, loan paperwork, and manufacturer registration forms are compiled by the dealership's documentation desk, minimising the number of separate visits required from the client.

**Step 8 — Registration (Day 20–35 for in-stock; varies for factory orders).** Temporary registration is arranged for immediate road use, with permanent RTO registration completed within the statutory window (typically within 30 days of temporary registration under Haryana RTO rules).

**Step 9 — Payment (prior to delivery).** Full and final payment (or the financed portion's disbursement) is settled before the vehicle leaves the dealership.

**Step 10 — Vehicle preparation (final 3–5 days before delivery).** The vehicle undergoes a pre-delivery inspection, detailing, fuel/charge top-up, and application of any pre-agreed protective coatings (PPF, ceramic coating) if ordered.

**Step 11 — Final delivery.** Delivery takes place either at the showroom (with an optional private delivery bay for flagship models) or, for corporate and select flagship clients, at the client's home or office.

**Step 12 — Ownership orientation.** At delivery, a dedicated orientation session (typically 45–90 minutes depending on vehicle complexity) walks the client through key systems, connected services setup, and scheduling of the first service.

**Typical end-to-end timelines:** in-stock vehicle, cash purchase: 2–3 weeks. In-stock vehicle with financing: 3–4 weeks. Factory-order mainstream brand (Porsche, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Land Rover): 3–7 months. Bentley factory orders also typically fall within this 3–7 month range (4–6 months per Section 2) despite being categorised as an exotic/ultra-luxury brand for booking-amount purposes (see Section 14). Certain high-demand models are a documented exception to the mainstream range regardless of brand tier — the Mercedes-AMG G63, for instance, typically carries a 10–14 month waitlist due to exceptional demand (see Section 2's Mercedes-Benz profile and Section 3's inventory listing). Factory-order exotic brand (Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Aston Martin): 6–10 months. Rolls-Royce bespoke commission: 8–12 months.

## 5. Financing

Velocity Luxe Motors' in-house finance desk works with five partner banks and NBFCs to arrange financing, though clients are always free to arrange independent financing if preferred.

**EMI and loan tenure.** Loan tenures range from 12 to 84 months depending on the lender and vehicle category. Most luxury vehicle loans in this segment are written for 36–60 months, balancing manageable EMI against typical ownership-and-upgrade cycles of 3–5 years.

**Down payment.** Standard down payment requirements range from 20% to 40% of on-road price, with luxury and exotic brand financing typically requiring the higher end of this range due to lender risk policies on rapid depreciation categories.

**Corporate finance.** Corporate and business-owner clients purchasing through a company entity can access commercial vehicle loan structures with different depreciation and tax treatment; the finance desk coordinates directly with the client's chartered accountant where needed.

**Leasing.** Operating lease options are available for corporate clients (typically 24–48 month terms) through two of the dealership's partner NBFCs, Kotak Mahindra Prime and Bajaj Finserv, popular for company-provided executive vehicles where the balance sheet treatment of leasing is preferred over ownership.

**Balloon payment.** Balloon payment (deferred final instalment) structures are available on select models, allowing a lower monthly EMI in exchange for a larger final payment or refinance/trade-in at term end — commonly used by clients who plan to upgrade vehicles every 3 years.

**Loan eligibility.** Eligibility is assessed on income documentation, existing liabilities, and credit history, following each partner lender's standard criteria; the finance desk provides an informal pre-qualification assessment within 24 hours of document submission.

**Required documents.** PAN card, Aadhaar card, income tax returns (typically last 2–3 years), bank statements (typically last 6 months), and for self-employed/business clients, GST returns and business financial statements.

**Processing time.** Standard processing time is 5–10 working days from complete document submission to sanction, though pre-approved clients with existing banking relationships to partner lenders may receive faster turnaround.

**Partner banks.** The dealership's finance desk maintains active partnerships with HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Kotak Mahindra Prime, and Bajaj Finserv for vehicle financing, updated periodically as lender terms change.

**Frequently asked questions.** Clients most commonly ask about interest rate ranges (variable by lender and credit profile, generally in the low-to-mid double digits annually for this vehicle category at time of writing), prepayment/foreclosure charges (varies by lender, typically nil-to-modest after 12 months), and whether financing is available on Certified Pre-Owned vehicles (yes, though loan-to-value ratios are typically slightly more conservative than for new vehicles).

## 6. Insurance

The dealership's insurance desk arranges coverage through three partner insurers — ICICI Lombard General Insurance, HDFC ERGO, and Tata AIG General Insurance — and can also process policies with a client's existing insurer of choice.

**Comprehensive insurance.** Standard comprehensive coverage is strongly recommended and, in practice, required by all financing partners as a condition of the loan.

**Zero depreciation.** Zero depreciation (nil-depreciation) add-on cover is recommended for all vehicles in this price category and included by default in the insurance desk's standard quotation, given the high cost of replacement parts.

**Third-party insurance.** Statutory third-party liability coverage is included in every policy as required by Indian motor insurance law, and is available on a standalone basis for clients who prefer to arrange own-damage coverage separately.

**Engine protection.** Engine and gearbox protection cover is recommended, particularly for SUV models used off-road (Land Rover, Range Rover) or in flood-prone monsoon conditions common to parts of the NCR region.

**Roadside assistance.** A 24/7 roadside assistance add-on is bundled with most policies arranged through the dealership; see Section 13 for the dealership's own roadside assistance service, which operates alongside insurer-provided roadside cover.

**Claim process.** In the event of a claim, clients are encouraged to contact the dealership's insurance desk directly, which coordinates directly with the insurer and the dealership's own body shop for cashless repair processing where the policy and repair facility both qualify.

**Insurance renewal.** The insurance desk sends renewal reminders 30 days ahead of policy expiry and can process renewal alongside a vehicle's scheduled service visit for client convenience.

## 7. Trade-In Programme

Velocity Luxe Motors accepts trade-ins of any make or model, not only vehicles originally purchased through the dealership, though trade-in valuations for brands represented in-house tend to be more precise due to greater market data.

**Vehicle evaluation.** An initial desktop valuation is provided within 24 hours based on make, model, year, and reported condition, followed by an in-person inspection to confirm the final offer.

**Inspection.** The in-person inspection covers exterior panel condition, interior wear, mechanical function, tyre condition, and a test drive by the dealership's evaluation technician, typically taking 60–90 minutes.

**Service history review.** Vehicles with a complete, dealership-stamped service history typically receive a stronger valuation than those with incomplete or third-party service records, reflecting resale market realities.

**Accident history.** Any prior accident or structural repair history must be disclosed and is verified where possible through service records and physical inspection (paint thickness measurement, panel alignment check); non-disclosure discovered post-evaluation can result in a revised offer.

**Ownership verification.** Registration certificate, insurance policy, and loan closure documentation (if applicable) are verified before an offer is finalised, ensuring clear title transfer.

**Market valuation.** The final offer reflects current wholesale market data for the specific make, model, year, mileage, and condition, cross-referenced against the dealership's own recent Certified Pre-Owned sales for comparable vehicles.

**Final offer.** A written offer, valid for 7 days, is provided following inspection; the client may accept the offer as a trade-in credit toward a new purchase or, in most cases, request outright purchase without a corresponding new vehicle purchase.

**Payment timeline.** Once accepted, trade-in payment (or credit application toward a new purchase) is processed within 3–5 working days, pending completion of ownership transfer paperwork.

## 8. Test Drives

**Booking process.** Test drives can be booked via phone, email, or in person, and are recommended to be scheduled at least 24 hours in advance to ensure the specific model and consultant availability.

**Documents required.** A valid driving licence is required for all test drives; for exotic and high-performance models (Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Aston Martin, Rolls-Royce, Bentley), a minimum of 2 years' licence history is requested, and a refundable security acknowledgement form is signed prior to the drive.

**Duration.** Standard test drives run 20–30 minutes on a pre-defined route covering both city and highway-speed conditions; extended test drives (up to 60 minutes) can be arranged for serious buyers by prior request.

**Home demonstration.** Home or office demonstration drives are available for flagship and exotic models, where a sales consultant brings the vehicle to the client's location for a supervised drive, subject to distance and scheduling.

**Chauffeur demonstration.** For models frequently purchased for chauffeur use (Rolls-Royce Phantom, Bentley Flying Spur, Mercedes-Benz S-Class, BMW 7 Series, Audi A8 L), a chauffeur-perspective demonstration is available, where the client experiences the vehicle from the rear seat while a trained dealership driver demonstrates the ride.

**Weekend bookings.** Weekend test drive slots are available and, due to demand, are recommended to be booked at least 48 hours in advance.

**High-performance vehicle restrictions.** For Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, and Aston Martin models, test drives are conducted with a sales consultant present in the vehicle at all times, and are restricted to pre-approved routes; unsupervised or track-condition test drives are not offered as standard (track experience days are offered separately to confirmed buyers post-purchase, per Section 2's brand sections).

**Cancellation policy.** Test drive bookings can be cancelled or rescheduled with no penalty up to 4 hours before the scheduled time; late cancellations are simply rescheduled at the consultant's discretion with no formal penalty, as no deposit is required to book a standard test drive.

## 9. Warranty

**Manufacturer warranty.** New vehicle warranty terms vary by brand as detailed in Section 3's inventory listings, generally ranging from 3 years (most brands) to 4 years (Rolls-Royce, Porsche), typically with unlimited mileage across all represented brands.

**Certified pre-owned warranty.** CPO vehicles carry the remaining balance of original manufacturer warranty where applicable, supplemented by a dealership-backed CPO warranty extension to ensure a minimum coverage period; exact remaining coverage is listed per vehicle in Section 3.

**Extended warranty.** Extended warranty contracts, extending coverage 1–3 years beyond the standard term, are available for purchase at or after delivery, and are particularly popular among Land Rover and high-mileage-usage clients.

**Service contracts.** Pre-paid maintenance/service contracts, covering scheduled service costs for 3–5 years, can be purchased at delivery, locking in service costs against future price increases.

**Roadside assistance.** Manufacturer roadside assistance is included with new vehicle warranty for the duration of the warranty period across all brands; the dealership's own 24/7 roadside assistance line (Section 13) supplements this and is available regardless of warranty status.

**Warranty exclusions.** Standard exclusions apply across all brands: damage from accidents, improper use, unauthorised modifications, use of non-genuine parts, and failure to maintain the manufacturer-specified service schedule. Wear-and-tear items (tyres, brake pads, wiper blades) are generally excluded from warranty coverage and are addressed as normal maintenance items.

## 10. Service Centre

Velocity Luxe Motors operates an in-house, multi-brand service centre adjacent to the main showroom campus, staffed by manufacturer-certified technicians for each represented brand.

**Scheduled maintenance.** Standard service intervals are generally 12 months or 10,000–15,000 km (whichever comes first), varying slightly by brand and model; the service desk sends automated reminders ahead of due dates.

**Diagnostics.** Full manufacturer-specification diagnostic equipment is maintained for each brand represented, avoiding the need to outsource diagnostic work to third parties.

**Software updates.** Over-the-air and in-service software updates are applied as released by manufacturers, particularly relevant for EQS, Taycan, and other technology-forward models with frequent infotainment and driver-assistance updates.

**Ceramic coating.** Professional ceramic coating application is offered in-house, with packages ranging from single-layer entry coatings to multi-layer premium coatings carrying multi-year durability warranties.

**Paint Protection Film (PPF).** Full-body and partial (front-end) PPF application is available, using imported film stock, typically carrying a 5–10 year manufacturer film warranty against yellowing.

**Detailing.** Interior and exterior detailing packages are available on a standalone basis or bundled with scheduled service visits.

**Wheel alignment.** Precision wheel alignment and balancing is performed using brand-specific calibration data, particularly important for performance models with adjustable suspension geometry.

**Battery replacement.** For hybrid and electric models (Taycan, EQS, 296 GTB, Artura), battery health diagnostics are performed at each service, with replacement handled through certified manufacturer channels when required.

**Genuine spare parts.** All service work uses genuine manufacturer parts sourced through official channels; the parts desk maintains a moderate stock of high-turnover items on-site to reduce wait times for common service needs.

**Pickup and drop service.** A complimentary pickup and drop service is available within a 25 km radius of the dealership for scheduled service appointments, with charges applying beyond that radius based on distance.

## 12. Delivery Process

**Local delivery.** Standard delivery for Gurugram/Delhi NCR clients takes place at the showroom, with an optional private delivery bay available for flagship and exotic models to ensure client privacy during handover.

**Nationwide delivery.** Vehicles can be delivered to clients outside the NCR region via enclosed car transport; typical transport time is 3–7 days depending on destination, with the dealership coordinating registration in the client's home state where required.

**Temporary registration.** Temporary registration is arranged immediately at delivery for local use, valid for 30 days under Haryana RTO rules, while permanent registration is processed.

**Permanent registration.** Permanent RTO registration is handled by the dealership's documentation desk on the client's behalf, with the registration certificate typically couriered to the client within 3–4 weeks of delivery.

**Insurance activation.** Insurance coverage is activated to be effective from the delivery date, with the policy document provided at handover or shortly after.

**Vehicle inspection.** A joint pre-delivery inspection is conducted with the client at handover, reviewing exterior condition, interior condition, and confirming all ordered features and accessories are correctly fitted.

**Final documentation.** All ownership documents — invoice, warranty booklet, insurance policy, loan documentation (if applicable), and accessory receipts — are compiled into a single delivery folder provided at handover.

**Home delivery.** Home delivery is available on request for clients unable to visit the showroom, most commonly used for flagship model deliveries where privacy is a priority.

**Corporate delivery.** Corporate fleet or company-registered vehicle deliveries can be scheduled at the client's office premises, with the documentation desk coordinating any additional corporate KYC requirements in advance.

## 14. Policies

**Reservation Policy.** A reservation may be placed on any in-stock or factory-order vehicle with payment of the applicable booking amount, securing the vehicle or production slot against sale to another client.

**Booking Amount Policy.** Booking amounts vary by vehicle category: ₹5 lakh for mainstream brands (Porsche, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Land Rover), ₹10 lakh for exotic/ultra-luxury brands (Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Aston Martin, Bentley), and ₹25 lakh for Rolls-Royce bespoke commissions. Booking amounts are adjusted against the final purchase price at delivery.

**Cancellation Policy.** Reservations may be cancelled at any time prior to production commencement (for factory orders) or vehicle preparation commencement (for in-stock vehicles) for a full refund of the booking amount less any documented administrative costs already incurred, which are limited to standard processing fees and typically do not exceed 5% of the booking amount. Once production or preparation has commenced, cancellation is subject to case-by-case review, as the dealership may already have incurred non-recoverable manufacturer costs.

**Refund Policy.** Approved refunds are processed within 10–15 working days to the original payment source. Refunds related to financing that has already been disbursed are coordinated directly with the lending institution and may take longer depending on the lender's own process.

**Vehicle Availability Policy.** In-stock vehicle availability is confirmed at the time of reservation, not merely at the time of enquiry, as showroom stock can change; the dealership does not guarantee availability based on a verbal enquiry alone.

**Test Drive Policy.** See Section 8 in full; a valid driving licence is mandatory for all test drives, and high-performance models require consultant supervision at all times.

**Trade-In Policy.** See Section 7 in full; trade-in offers are valid for 7 days from issuance and are subject to re-inspection if not accepted within that window.

**Privacy Policy.** Client personal and financial information is used solely for the purposes of completing the purchase, financing, insurance, and ongoing service relationship, and is not shared with third parties outside these purposes without client consent, except where required by law.

**Data Protection Policy.** Client documents and data are stored securely with access restricted to relevant staff on a need-to-know basis, and retained only as long as required for transaction, warranty, and statutory record-keeping purposes.

**Financing Disclaimer.** Financing terms, interest rates, and approval are subject to the lending institution's own credit policy and are not guaranteed by Velocity Luxe Motors; the dealership acts as a facilitator between client and lender, not as the lender itself.

**Warranty Disclaimer.** Warranty coverage and terms are set by the respective vehicle manufacturer; Velocity Luxe Motors administers claims on the manufacturer's behalf but does not independently extend or alter manufacturer warranty terms beyond the dealership-backed CPO and extended warranty products explicitly described in Section 9.

**Vehicle Pricing Disclaimer.** All prices quoted are ex-showroom, Gurugram, and exclude registration, insurance, and any optional accessories or customisation unless explicitly stated; prices are subject to change based on manufacturer price revisions, exchange rate movements, and applicable duty changes, and the price applicable at the time of final invoicing governs the transaction.
